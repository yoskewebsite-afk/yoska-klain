## Motion and interaction

- Hero: grouped 550ms entrances with 55ms offsets, then modest depth on desktop scroll.
- Landscape: 180svh wrapper including its viewport; 1.02–1.055 scale and 42px landscape travel. Separate authentic sky crop and feathered atmospheric boundary layers. No use of old inpainted city layers.
- Planning: 170svh wrapper including its viewport; SVG stroke progress, moving waypoint and sequential shallow perspective settlement.
- People photos settle from different offsets; no perpetual animation.
- One event-driven native-scroll scheduler; geometry refreshed after fonts, image loads and resize. Scroll progress is immediate and reversible.
- Mobile converts both sticky scenes into normal flow and the process into a vertical sequence. Reduced motion shows final content without spatial travel or pinning.
- Tour cards support visible controls and native RTL horizontal touch scrolling. Accordions support keyboard controls and meaningful expanded states.
- The matcher preserves answers when going back, produces an existing-tour suggestion, and carries all preferences into an editable WhatsApp draft.
- The contact form validates phone/name, previews the exact enquiry, and opens WhatsApp only on the visitor's separate action. No backend storage or automatic message transmission.
